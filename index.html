About Limise

Built on a decade in skills development, and a conviction that a scorecard should change a life.

SN

Songezo Ntinge

Co-Founder & Managing Director

A decade-experienced practitioner in the skills development sector. Songezo founded Limise on a simple belief: every rand invested in workplace learning should produce a measurable return — for the organisation that spent it, and for the person whose life it changed.

TV

Thembakazi Veyishile

Co-Founder & Director

A more than decade-experienced HR practitioner with strong experience in the skills development sector, grounding Limise's work in what HR teams actually need to succeed.

Mission

To empower South African organisations to transform their skills development obligations into measurable talent outcomes, combining rigorous compliance management, evidence-based learning design, and a deep commitment to developing the country's youth workforce.

Vision

A South Africa where every rand invested in workplace learning produces a measurable return, for the organisation that spent it, and for the person whose life it changed.

Our Services, In Full

What each engagement includes.

Service 01

Skills Development Facilitation (SDF) & SETA Compliance

WSP/ATR submission, mandatory and discretionary grant recovery, SETA portal management, and B-BBEE skills development scorecard evidence — the compliance foundation every mid-market company needs but few manage well. For HR Directors and L&D Managers who need it handled correctly, on deadline, every cycle.

Book a discovery call →
Service 02

Learning Architecture & Evaluation

Training needs analysis, learning programme design, LMS advisory, and a structured 4-level evaluation framework — reaction, learning, behaviour, and business results — so every training investment can be measured at the level that matters. For CFOs and CEOs who want evidence, not soft L&D messaging.

Book a discovery call →
Service 03

Learnership & Internship Programme Management

End-to-end project management of learnership and internship cohorts: recruitment and screening, SETA registration, mentor matching, progress reporting, and B-BBEE absorption evidence. Built so the programme runs itself, and reports itself.

Book a discovery call →
Service 04

Learner Support, Mentoring & Coaching

Structured mentoring cadence, "train the mentor" workshops for line managers, an early-warning system for at-risk learners, and career-readiness coaching approaching programme completion. Dignity and aspiration for the person on the other end of the compliance line item.

Book a discovery call →
Service 05

HR Network & Knowledge Hub

A private HR practitioner community, monthly webinars and masterclasses, a weekly newsletter, and a digital resource library — WSP/ATR templates, evaluation toolkits, B-BBEE scorecard trackers.

Explore resources →
